Questions

What people ask first.

Still unsure? A real person picks up the phone, Monday to Saturday.

Call (555) 016-3318
Lena from Fairview asks…Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly. If you have a family doctor, we'll send them a copy of your summary whenever you ask us to.

Tom from Westbrook asks…Does Tamsa replace my family doctor?

No, we work alongside them. We focus on prevention and long visits. Your family doctor stays your first stop when you're ill, and for prescriptions and referrals.

Aiko from Fairview asks…What does "functional medicine" mean here?

Looking at the whole picture — sleep, food, stress, movement and family history — next to standard, well-established tests. We don't sell supplements and we don't offer unproven treatments.

Sam from Millbrook asks…Can I use my insurance?

We don't bill insurers directly. You get an itemised receipt to send to your insurer yourself, and some plans pay part of it back.

Grace from Fairview asks…Can I pause or cancel my membership?

Yes. Monthly plans can be paused or cancelled with 30 days' notice. Yearly plans are refunded for the full months you haven't used.

Omar from Westbrook asks…How do I prepare for the first visit?

Eat as usual unless we tell you otherwise, bring any results from the past year, and wear something with sleeves that roll up easily.
